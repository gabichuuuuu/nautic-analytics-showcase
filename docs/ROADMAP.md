# Public roadmap

Status reviewed: 19 September 2026.

This roadmap describes the private development project, not a released product or a promise of delivery dates. "In place" means present in the development workflow; it does not imply complete physical validation or public availability.

[Project overview](../README.md) · [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md) · [Notice](../NOTICE.md)

## Foundations — in place

- local application shell for vessel, environment and mooring inputs;
- calculation workflow with component catalogues;
- engineering checks and utilization views;
- regression-oriented validation of the calculation model;
- PDF and Excel technical outputs;
- an individual-buoy workspace joining requirements, geometry, component selection and editable engineering detail.

## In active development

- clearer traceability from assumptions and declared capacities to results and unresolved checks;
- broader verification of model behaviour, boundary cases and applicable limits;
- refinement of the unified buoy workspace and the distinction between proposals and evaluated detail;
- more consistent technical-report and material-list presentation.

## Public demonstration — planned

- generalized examples using synthetic inputs and explicit assumptions;
- selected visuals that explain the workflow without exposing private material;
- a documented boundary between demonstrated behaviour and remaining engineering validation.

No interactive public demo is currently provided by this repository. Sharing examples depends on reviewing their content and limitations first.

## Later

- selected reusable utilities, if and when the open-source boundary is defined;
- feedback-driven improvements to the review workflow.

## Intentionally excluded from this public roadmap

Client schedules, project identifiers, private deliverables, confidential calculations and unreleased implementation details. They remain in the private engineering workspace.
